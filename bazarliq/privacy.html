Bazarliq — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026
Privacy Support Manual

This Privacy Policy explains how Bazarliq ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Bazarliq is a shopping-list, recipe-exchange, and bargain-locator app for the home shopper. We collect only what is needed to run the app and we do not sell your personal data.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in (Google, Apple, or email), we receive your display name and email address to create and secure your account.
  • Your content. Shopping lists, items, groups, quantities, and any lists you share with other people you invite.
  • Microphone (voice input). If you use the "add by voice" feature, your speech is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition to convert it into text. We do not record or store audio.
  • Basic technical data. Standard diagnostic and authentication data needed to keep the service working and secure.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To store and sync your shopping lists across your devices.
  • To enable list sharing with people you explicitly invite.
  • To convert your spoken words into list items when you use voice input.
  • To maintain security and improve reliability of the app.

How your data is stored

Bazarliq uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store your account and list data securely in the cloud. Your data is protected by access rules so that only you — and members you invite to a shared list — can read or edit it.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only:

  • With other users you personally invite to a shared shopping list.
  • With our infrastructure provider, Google Firebase, solely to operate the app (see Google's privacy terms).
  • Where required by law.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, and we will remove it.

Children's privacy

Bazarliq is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
