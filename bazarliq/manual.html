Privacy Support Manual

Bazarliq is a shopping companion for the home shopper: build smart shopping lists, add items by voice, share lists with family, browse recipes, and find the best bazaar prices near you. This guide covers every feature currently in the app and is updated as the app evolves.

1. Getting started & signing in

  • Choose your language from the button at the top-right of the sign-in screen. Bazarliq supports Azerbaijani, English, Russian, and Turkish. You can change it later in Profile.
  • Sign in options: Email & password (tap "Create account" to sign up), Continue with Google, Continue with Facebook, or Continue with Apple.
  • Your account keeps your lists synced and backed up in the cloud, and lets you share lists with others.

2. Getting around

The bottom bar has four tabs:

  • List — your shopping lists.
  • Recipes — recipe ideas with cost estimates.
  • Bazaar — community prices and vendor map.
  • Profile — account, language, and settings.

3. Shopping lists

  • Estimated total appears at the top of the list, based on the latest bazaar prices, along with the item count.
  • Add an item: tap the Add product button, then search the catalog or add a custom item by typing its name.
  • AI suggestions: quick-add chips suggest products you may want, based on your list.
  • Add by voice: use the microphone to speak your items (see section 4).
  • Check off items with the checkbox as you shop.
  • Multiple lists: use the menu (⋮) in the top bar to create a New list and switch between lists.

4. Adding items by voice

  • Tap the microphone, allow microphone access, and speak your items (e.g. "tomatoes, bread, two kilos of rice").
  • Bazarliq converts speech to text on your device and matches it to products; recognized items appear as chips.
  • Remove any chip you didn't mean, then tap Add to put them on the list.
  • Voice works in your selected app language. Audio is never recorded or stored.

5. Editing an item (quantity, unit & group)

  • Tap an item to open the editor.
  • Quantity / weight: use the + / − buttons or type a value directly.
  • Unit: choose kg, gram, piece, bunch, litre, or pack.
  • Group: assign the item to a group such as Vegetables, Fruits, Meat, Fish & seafood, Dairy, Bakery, Grocery, Spices, Beverages, Frozen, Snacks, Household, or Other.
  • Create a new group: tap + New group and type a name (e.g. "Cleaning", "Baby"). It becomes reusable for other items.

6. Grouping & organization

  • Items are automatically grouped under headers (Vegetables, Fruits, Meat, etc.) so the list mirrors how you shop.
  • Built-in groups appear first in a fixed order, your custom groups next, and Other last.

7. Removing items

  • Swipe an item left to delete it, or
  • tap the item and choose Remove from list in the editor.

8. Sharing a list

  • Share: tap the share icon on a list. Bazarliq creates an invite code you can copy or send via SMS.
  • Join a shared list: open the menu (⋮) → Join a shared list, then enter the invite code someone shared with you.
  • Real-time: everyone on a shared list sees changes instantly — great for a couple or family coordinating one shopping run.
  • Members are shown on the shared list.
  • Leave: open the menu (⋮) → Leave shared list to stop sharing.

9. Recipes

  • Browse recipes with a short description, number of servings, and an estimated cost based on current bazaar prices.
  • Tap a recipe to view full details, ingredients, and step-by-step instructions.
  • Add a recipe's ingredients to your shopping list in one step.

10. Bazaar — prices & vendors

  • Prices tab: see community-reported prices per product, with the cheapest highlighted and how fresh each report is. Tap 👍 to agree with a price.
  • Report a price: tap Report price, pick the product and bazaar, enter the price and unit, and optionally add a quality note.
  • Vendors tab: view vendors on a map with their stall, bazaar, and rating. Tap the location icon to focus a vendor on the map.

11. Profile & settings

  • I'm a vendor: toggle this if you sell at a bazaar.
  • My Lists: tap to jump to your shopping lists; the badge shows how many you have.
  • Language: switch between Azerbaijani, English, Russian, and Turkish anytime.
  • Sign out from the bottom of the Profile screen.
