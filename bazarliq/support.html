Common questions

  • How do I add items by voice? Open a list, tap the voice button, and speak the products. Bazarliq turns your speech into list items.
  • How do I share a list? Open a list, tap the share icon, and send the invite code to the person you want to share with.
  • How do I delete an item? Swipe an item left, or tap it and choose "Remove from list".
  • How do I delete my account or data? Email us at pryroinc@gmail.com and we'll remove your account and associated data.
